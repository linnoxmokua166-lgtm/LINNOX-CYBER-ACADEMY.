Lesson 1: What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and information from digital threats.

  • Personal information
  • Business information
  • Computer systems
  • Online accounts
  • Digital communication

Lesson 2: The Three Security Principles

1. Confidentiality

Ensuring information is only accessible to authorized people.

2. Integrity

Keeping information accurate, complete and trustworthy.

3. Availability

Ensuring systems and information can be accessed when needed.

Lesson 3: Common Cybersecurity Threats

  • Phishing: Deceptive messages designed to trick people.
  • Malware: Software designed to cause harm or unwanted activity.
  • Ransomware: Malware that locks or encrypts information and demands payment.
  • Social engineering: Manipulating people into revealing information.
  • Data breaches: Unauthorized exposure of private information.

Lesson 4: Basic Online Safety

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
  • Never share your login credentials.
  • Back up important information.

Lesson 5: Ethical Cybersecurity

Ethical cybersecurity means protecting systems responsibly and respecting other people's privacy.

Always obtain permission before testing a computer system, website or network.

Never access another person's accounts or systems without authorization.
